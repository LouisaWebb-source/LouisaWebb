THE CELESTIAL DOMINION

Nine Hosts. One Dominion endures.

The Celestial Dominion is a vast terrestrial realm south of the Fae and Dragon Empires, divided into nine Hosts with the Palace of Light standing on neutral ground at its political centre.

General Geography

The Dominion lies between the Sea of Serenity in the west, the Lucent Sea in the east and the Silvermere Strait along much of its southern coast. A great mountain spine divides much of the northern interior, while the south contains forests, river valleys, wetlands and coastal lands.

Its political principle is summarised as: Seven Minor Hosts uphold the realms. Two Major Hosts guide the whole. One Dominion endures.

The Two Major Hosts

Host of Radiant Judgment: centred on Luminor and traditionally associated with law, judgment, government and institutional authority.

Host of Eternal Harmony: centred on Harmonia and associated with unity, balance, moderation and cooperation between institutions.

The Seven Minor Hosts

Silver Flame: oaths, duty and purity of purpose.

Dawning Wind: high country, skies and horizons.

Moonlit Veil: secrets, passage and secluded western valleys.

Celestial Tide: maritime trade, navigation and waterways.

Stardust Seekers: exploration, scholarship and discovery.

Evergreen Grove: life, renewal, forests and fertile valleys.

Stonebound Sentinel: mountains, stonework, fortifications and endurance.

The Palace of Light

The Palace of Light occupies neutral ground between the two Major Hosts and serves as the political heart of the Dominion. It is more than a royal residence: it is also the physical manifestation of Lumen Caecans, the sentient entity whose boundaries encompass the Celestial Dominion.
