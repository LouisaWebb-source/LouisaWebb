CHARACTERS & ALLIANCES

Trust is dangerous. So is wanting it.

Fairyland is crowded with emperors, warriors, scholars, rivals and chosen family. Every relationship changes the balance of power—and nobody arrives without a history.

CENTRAL FIGURES

Meet them at the beginning

Only what a new reader can safely know before entering Book One.

THE HEROINE

Franziska “Franzi”

A practical adult woman from Earth whose quiet Christmas plans end in abduction. Dryly funny, stubborn and difficult to intimidate, Franzi enters Fairyland with no intention of becoming anyone’s obedient captive.

Outsider · Survivor · Disruptive influence

THE DEMON EMPEROR

Damian Roseheart

An Incubus Emperor first encountered as a powerless prisoner. Intelligent, formal, politically dangerous and unexpectedly amused by Franzi, Damian gives her magical powers—and changes the course of both their lives.

Emperor · Sire · House Roseheart

FOUND FAMILY

House Roseheart

A formidable Demon house made up of warriors, scholars, diplomats and organisers. Affection and danger sit comfortably beside one another, and family is defined as much by choice as by blood.

Family · Court · Political power

THE WIDER WORLD

Rulers, rivals and uneasy allies

Fairyland’s emperors, monarchs and courts bring old conflicts to every meeting. An enemy may become an ally, an ally may want a price, and neutrality rarely survives first contact with Franzi.

Courts · Realms · Shifting loyalties

HOW CONNECTIONS WORK

Not every bond means the same thing

Fairyland makes relationships personal, political and sometimes magical.

Blood

Lineage carries inheritance, expectation and old obligations.

Choice

Chosen family can become more binding than ancestry.

Duty

Titles, courts and empires turn private loyalties into public consequences.

Magic

Some connections can be felt as power—but their meaning is never simple.
