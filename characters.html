House Roseheart
Damian Roseheart

Role: Demon Emperor and Franzi’s Sire; ruler of the Demon Empire.

Race: Incubus.

Age: app. 300 years

Appearance: Strikingly handsome and strongly built, with an aristocratic presence even while chained naked in a dungeon. Once restored to normal life, he is usually impeccably dressed and groomed in perfectly fitted court or Imperial clothing.

Face: Sharp cheekbones, strong jaw, full lips, dark brows and a hint of stubble. His extraordinary bright blue eyes resemble blue diamonds and can glow with blue light when he uses or carries significant magic. Under extreme infernal power or fury, darkness can temporarily swallow the blue until his eyes become completely black

Hair: Long, dark to black hair.

Body Build: Broad-shouldered, muscular and powerful.

Voice: Deep and melodic, with a faint accent Franzi cannot initially place. His speech is naturally formal and somewhat antiquated, reflecting his aristocratic upbringing and age, but he shifts easily into dry humour and teasing affection. With Franzi his voice is often warm, gentle or amused, particularly when calling her “treasure”, while his Imperial authority emerges as a quiet, unmistakable command when necessary.

Personality: Intelligent, confident, affectionate, teasing and intensely protective of his family. He possesses considerable political cunning and ruthless authority when required, but with Franzi he frequently displays humour, patience and open pride in her. He is observant, emotionally perceptive and capable of moving almost seamlessly between affectionate Sire, experienced lover and Demon Emperor.

Mating / hareem bond: Mate-bonded to Franzi; he is not one of her hareem bonds. Their bond initially appears as a shadowy connection. When Franzi openly acknowledges their connection, the bond transforms into deep crimson, glittering with tiny ruby-bright points like gemstones caught in fire. As a mating bond, it is woven into the soul rather than merely attached to the magical core.

Sexual energy: Tastes of strong black coffee balanced by caramelised sugar, aged leather, the sensation of home and parchment filled with ancient stories, with dangerous metal running beneath it all. Powerful, grounding and unmistakably Damian.

Magical energy: Deeply familiar to Franzi because her own magical system was originally created from Damian’s power. His signature carries the warmth and certainty of coming home, together with the loose-limbed contentment of lying in bed after exceptionally good sex. Beneath that comfort lies a darker, richer sensation: a deep velvet pulse threaded with controlled heat and a faint metallic weight suggesting tremendous contained power. To Franzi, it feels safe, sinful and unmistakably Damian.

Nick for Franzi: Treasure

Sexual energy: Tastes of meat sizzling over hot coals, a blazing sunset and a metallic undertone of immense power, hotter, darker and more primal than Aurelian’s.

Magical energy: Dense, hot and ancient, like standing too close to a forge while something vast breathes behind the flames. Even a tiny sample carries an enormous sense of contained power.

Nickname / address for Franzi: Miracle, frequently my Miracle.

Kasimir Luthien

Role: Grand Magister Kasimir Luthien, First Dragon Wizard, one of the most powerful and respected Dragon mages, and Serena Luthien’s father. Later material also establishes him as leader of the Circle of Eight.

Once Franzi heals him and restores his magical reserves, the apparent age falls away and he looks dramatically younger and strikingly attractive, approximately in his early thirties.

Sexual energy: Not specifically tasted or characterised by Franzi in Book 1.
