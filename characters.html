House Roseheart
Damian Roseheart

Role: Demon Emperor and Franzi’s Sire; ruler of the Demon Empire.

Race: Incubus.

Age: app. 300 years

Appearance: Strikingly handsome and strongly built, with an aristocratic presence even while chained naked in a dungeon. Once restored to normal life, he is usually impeccably dressed and groomed in perfectly fitted court or Imperial clothing.

Face: Sharp cheekbones, strong jaw, full lips, dark brows and a hint of stubble. His extraordinary bright blue eyes resemble blue diamonds and can glow with blue light when he uses or carries significant magic. Under extreme infernal power or fury, darkness can temporarily swallow the blue until his eyes become completely black

Hair: Long, dark to black hair.

Body Build: Broad-shouldered, muscular and powerful.

Voice: Deep and melodic, with a faint accent Franzi cannot initially place. His speech is naturally formal and somewhat antiquated, reflecting his aristocratic upbringing and age, but he shifts easily into dry humour and teasing affection. With Franzi his voice is often warm, gentle or amused, particularly when calling her “treasure”, while his Imperial authority emerges as a quiet, unmistakable command when necessary.

Personality: Intelligent, confident, affectionate, teasing and intensely protective of his family. He possesses considerable political cunning and ruthless authority when required, but with Franzi he frequently displays humour, patience and open pride in her. He is observant, emotionally perceptive and capable of moving almost seamlessly between affectionate Sire, experienced lover and Demon Emperor.

Mating / hareem bond: Mate-bonded to Franzi; he is not one of her hareem bonds. Their bond initially appears as a shadowy connection. When Franzi openly acknowledges their connection, the bond transforms into deep crimson, glittering with tiny ruby-bright points like gemstones caught in fire. As a mating bond, it is woven into the soul rather than merely attached to the magical core.

Sexual energy: Tastes of strong black coffee balanced by caramelised sugar, aged leather, the sensation of home and parchment filled with ancient stories, with dangerous metal running beneath it all. Powerful, grounding and unmistakably Damian.

Magical energy: Deeply familiar to Franzi because her own magical system was originally created from Damian’s power. His signature carries the warmth and certainty of coming home, together with the loose-limbed contentment of lying in bed after exceptionally good sex. Beneath that comfort lies a darker, richer sensation: a deep velvet pulse threaded with controlled heat and a faint metallic weight suggesting tremendous contained power. To Franzi, it feels safe, sinful and unmistakably Damian.

Nick for Franzi: Treasure

Sexual energy: Tastes of meat sizzling over hot coals, a blazing sunset and a metallic undertone of immense power, hotter, darker and more primal than Aurelian’s.

Magical energy: Dense, hot and ancient, like standing too close to a forge while something vast breathes behind the flames. Even a tiny sample carries an enormous sense of contained power.

Nickname / address for Franzi: Miracle, frequently my Miracle.

Kasimir Luthien

Role: Grand Magister Kasimir Luthien, First Dragon Wizard, one of the most powerful and respected Dragon mages, and Serena Luthien’s father. Later material also establishes him as leader of the Circle of Eight.

Once Franzi heals him and restores his magical reserves, the apparent age falls away and he looks dramatically younger and strikingly attractive, approximately in his early thirties.

Sexual energy: Not specifically tasted or characterised by Franzi in Book 1.

His movements are fluid and precise, with almost no wasted motion, making combat resemble a lethal form of dance. His blades and his magic are designed to function together, which is why Franzi’s energy weapons would not be an improvement for him.

Skills: Swordsmanship, infiltration, intelligence gathering, court navigation, diplomacy, deception, charm and covert protection. Thorn is particularly valuable as a bodyguard because he can remain beside Franzi in a ballroom, diplomatic meeting or royal court without looking like conventional security. He can collect information while protecting her and has contacts and knowledge unavailable to more obvious military guards.

Personality: Charming, intelligent, polished, irreverent and morally grey. Thorn understands etiquette extremely well and therefore knows precisely how far he can bend it without technically breaking it. He has a sardonic sense of humour, enjoys provoking reactions and frequently hides serious observation behind apparent amusement. He is capable of analysing almost anything as a tactical problem, including entertainment.

His charm should not be mistaken for carelessness. Thorn is extremely observant, experienced and dangerous. When the situation becomes serious, the playfulness can disappear almost instantly.

Relationship with Carfaxx: Thorn is one of Carfaxx Nightfall’s closest friends. Carfaxx trusts him with his life, and recommending Thorn as Franzi’s bodyguard is one of the first moments that forces Franzi to recognise that Carfaxx possesses considerable judgement, professional knowledge and relationships outside his role as her Stallion.

Relationship with Franzi: Thorn is drawn to Franzi partly because of what she did during the Facility rescues, but he explicitly refuses to frame his service as repayment. His position is simple: he does not protect her because he owes her. He protects her because he chooses to.

He also recognises that Franzi is already standing in the middle of increasingly dangerous political events and offers to stand there with her rather than pretending he can keep her away from them.

Spoiler Backstory: Thorn was born Vaeli Aelvaraen, the youngest son of a declining minor noble House of the Twilight Court. House Aelvaraen traditionally produced diplomats, intelligence brokers and contractual blades, placing information, court influence and carefully applied violence at the centre of the family’s power.

As the House declined, Thorn’s older brother entered into a protection agreement with a rising criminal syndicate involved in forbidden magic, memory manipulation and soul-bound assassins. What began as an attempt to preserve the House gradually pulled the family deeper into criminal activity, including laundering favours, selling information and providing access to noble circles.

Vaeli became collateral.

While still training as a Blade Singer, he was taken by the syndicate, stripped of his former identity and subjected to magic designed to erase his name and restrict what he could reveal. A binding silence spell was placed upon him, and he was turned into a shadowblade assassin whose elegant Blade Singer training made him frighteningly effective.

The identity Thorn emerged from that period, while Vaeli Aelvaraen effectively disappeared.

His assignments became increasingly clean, quiet and feared until he was eventually imprisoned in the third Facility. There, everything that had once made him dangerous meant very little. He describes himself as having become nobody inside the Facility.

Franzi’s attack on the Facility changed that.

She did not rescue Lord Aelvaraen, a Blade Singer or a useful intelligence operative. She rescued prisoners because they were prisoners.

That distinction matters enormously to Thorn and is part of why, once free, he eventually chooses to stand beside her.
