BONDED BY FAIRYLAND

Book TwoAdult dark portal romantasyIn development

Franzi survived being abducted into Fairyland.

Now she has to survive belonging to it.

She has a new body, dangerous new instincts, a growing collection of bonds she barely understands—and an entire world beginning to look to her for answers she does not have.

Then the Wolven are attacked.

Someone inside the Demon Army is using Imperial soldiers to target Wolven villages, and the real objective is far worse: capture the Wolven King and Queen and tear their people apart from within. For Hunter, the Wolven Crown Prince who fought beside Franzi through the Facilities, the war has suddenly become painfully personal.

Franzi refuses to stand aside.

But while she helps the Wolven evacuate their people, shelters them inside a sentient Demonic Forest and searches for the traitor behind the attacks, an older danger begins to surface.

The Void.

Ancient songs tell of creatures that once consumed everything in their path—life, land and even the magic sustaining it. The Trollkin fought them. The Dwarves remember them. And if the old stories are true, Fairyland may be facing an enemy that cannot be bargained with, frightened off or allowed to spread.

Naturally, Franzi’s first thought is to try talking to it.

Unfortunately, Fairyland has apparently decided that becoming a Succubus, navigating increasingly complicated bonds, protecting an endangered people and preventing a civil war were not enough problems for one woman.

And the deeper Franzi digs into the Wolven attacks and the history of the Void, the clearer one thing becomes:

The monsters threatening Fairyland may already be inside its borders.

Bonded by Fairyland is Book Two of Fairyland is not what you think it is, a dark portal romantasy filled with dangerous magic, political betrayal, Wolven warriors, ancient horrors, morally questionable decisions and an expanding why-choose romance.
