Days 1–2 | Newborn Hunger and the First Full Night

Carfaxx’s Feeding Ledger

Subject: Franziska Roseheart

Period: Days 1–2

Day 1 · 22 Wintercrest

Her Imperial Highness

◆ Earlier in the day — Damian · Feeding source recorded by Serena. Exact clock period is not stated. This occurs during Franzi’s first hours as a Succubus, when Damian explains that sexual energy is nourishment and that a newborn Succubus may need to feed several times a day.

◆ Night / Overnight — Drago and Aurelian · Sustained feeding over several hours. Franzi repeatedly takes in sexual energy from both men and eventually falls asleep warm, full and deeply satisfied.

Energy state: Newborn capacity; unstable requirements → Full / sated by the end of the night.

Carfaxx assessment: First day establishes the problem immediately: she can become hungry quickly, but she can also take in a substantial amount when given enough time. Storage capacity is still that of a newborn Succubus. “She was full yesterday” is not a feeding plan.

Erica: Not yet applicable.

Day 2 · 23 Wintercrest

Her Imperial Highness

◆ Evening / Night — Noctyralyn · Serena records Noctyralyn as the day’s feeding source.

◆ Franzi begins the encounter starving. Noctyralyn states she suspects her Imperial Highness, the hunger becomes manageable, but she explicitly recognises that she is not fully sated.

Energy state: Starving → Functional, but not full.

Carfaxx assessment: Important distinction. Functional is not the same as adequately fed. Given the amount of Healing, Portalling and general catastrophe-management performed that day, this should be treated as a recovery feeding, not proof that her intake was sufficient.

Days 3–4 | Angel Energy and the Roseheart Family

Carfaxx’s Feeding Ledger

Subject: Franziska Roseheart

Period: Days 3–4

Day 3 · 24 Wintercrest

Franzi

◆ Late Night / Overnight — General Michael · Serena records General Michael and confirms that Franzi reaches the end of the rescue starving and Angelstruck. Michael removes her from the Facility and the feeding continues across the night. By the following morning she wakes relaxed, pleasantly used and thoroughly fed.

Energy state: Starving / judgement becoming compromised → Thoroughly fed.

Carfaxx assessment: This is precisely the state I do not want repeated. Hunger has progressed far enough to affect judgement and behaviour before someone intervenes. Angel energy is potent, but potency is irrelevant if she waits until she is desperate.

Day 4 · 25 Wintercrest

Franzi

◆ Evening / Night — Damian and the Roseheart stepsiblings · This is Serena’s collective entry in column K. The manuscript presents the encounter as a House-family feeding session in Damian’s apartments. The clearest direct feeding shown on the page is from Simon, while Damian and the wider Roseheart family are present and participating throughout the evening. Franzi ultimately falls asleep fed, warm, accepted and safe, and is described as fully sated for the moment.

Energy state: Hungry / recovering from the previous day’s exertion → Fully sated for the night.

Carfaxx assessment: Serena’s shorthand and the manuscript describe the same evening at different levels. I am retaining her collective “Damian and stepsiblings” notation while identifying Simon as the clearest direct source in the manuscript. This ledger is for health, not for pretending every group encounter can be reduced to one tidy name.

Days 5–6 | Bookshops, Trolls and Franzi’s Magical Forest

Carfaxx’s Feeding Ledger

Subject: Franziska Roseheart

Period: Days 5–6

Day 5 · 26 Wintercrest

Franzi

◆ Daytime / shopping trip — Master Edward Alisteir · Simon reports Master Edward Alasteir as the day’s feeding source. The encounter happened during the shopping trip, after Franzi is already very hungry. Edward provides a direct feeding, but it is not the only intake that day.

◆ Daytime — Simon · Small supplemental feeding. Simon’s release takes the edge off only briefly; Franzi herself effectively treats it as an appetiser rather than a meal.

◆ Daytime — Master Edward Alisteir, non-sexual passion · An unusual but useful observation: the intensity of their haggling produces passionate energy that Franzi can take in. I am recording it because the Succubus does not appear to care whether useful nourishing emotion arrived through a conventional route.

◆ Later daytime / early evening — Smasher the Troll · Major feeding. Smasher carries an extraordinary reserve after a very long period without sexual release. Franzi explicitly recognises herself as sated for the first time since waking after feeding from him.

Energy state: Very hungry / starving → briefly improved → fully sated after Smasher. Hunger begins returning again later that night.

Carfaxx assessment: Serena’s single-name entry is useful as a continuity marker, but wholly inadequate as a health record. Three direct sources plus incidental passionate energy were involved, and she still began getting hungry again before the day was over. Newborn capacity remains the principal problem. Also: if a Troll with a century of accumulated energy is required to produce a reliable “full”, we should perhaps stop treating one ordinary encounter as a daily ration.

Day 6 · 27 Wintercrest

Franzi

◆ Day / Night — Franzi’s Magical Forest, a Sentient Demonic Forest · Franzi enters the forest already hungry. Her official account is that she spent the day meditating and communicating with the Forest while Healing its old injuries. Whatever form that communication took, her Succubus clearly drew nourishment from the connection; by the end of the first cycle she explicitly recognises that she has fed.

Energy state: Hungry on entry → Fed. Exact fullness not stated.

Carfaxx assessment: The Princess insists that she meditated and communicated with a Sentient Demonic Forest. This is the wording I have therefore entered into the official health ledger. I have several professional qualifications and sufficient survival instinct not to interrogate the phrase further. What matters medically is that the sustained magical connection nourished her.

Days 7–8 | Meditation, Communication and an Imperial Nightcap

Carfaxx’s Feeding Ledger

Subject: Franziska Roseheart

Period: Days 7–8

Day 7 · 28 Wintercrest

Franzi

◆ All day into Night — Franzi’s Magical Forest, a Sentient Demonic Forest · Second day in the Forest. Franzi continues to describe the experience as meditation and communication with said Forest. The magical exchange is nevertheless substantial enough that her Succubus feeds very heavily; Franzi judges the resulting intake as more energy than the earlier encounter with Damian and her stepsiblings generated.

Energy state: Fed → Very heavily fed.

Carfaxx assessment: This is the first truly substantial reserve-building feeding in several days. The Forest is plainly capable of supplying extraordinary quantities of energy. The Princess continues to maintain that she was merely meditating and communicating. I have recorded her statement without comment, which I hope will be appreciated as the act of restraint it is.

Day 8 · 29 Wintercrest

Franzi

◆ Daytime — Franzi’s Magical Forest, a Sentient Demonic Forest · Third and quieter day. Franzi continues her meditation and communication with the Forest, and the magical connection remains nourishing. When she finally leaves, she is starving for ordinary food rather than energy, strongly indicating that her Succubus reserves remain adequate.

◆ Night / Overnight — Damian · Serena’s shorthand records the Forest / Damian for the day. After returning from the Forest, Franzi later exchanges energy with Damian and spends the night with him in a slow, sustained feeding encounter.

Energy state: Well fed on leaving the Forest → Maintained / reinforced overnight with Damian.

Carfaxx assessment: Finally, a day on which she does not wait until her Succubus is howling before feeding. The official record will therefore state: meditation, communication, excellent energy intake. Anything beyond that remains entirely between Franzi and her Sentient Demonic Forest.

Erica: Not yet applicable.

Days 9–10 | Commander Recovery and a Three-Meal Day

Carfaxx’s Feeding Ledger

Subject: Franziska Roseheart

Period: Days 9–10

Day 9 · 30 Wintercrest

Franzi

◆ Night — Commander Azrith Doomspire · Serena records Commander Azrith Doomspire / Nocty. The manuscript shows Franzi exhausted enough that she initially wants sleep despite her Succubus wanting to feed. She sleeps against Azrith, wakes starving, warns him, and then feeds from him during the night.

◆ Continuity note — Noctyralyn · Serena’s shorthand places Nocty on Day 9, but the manuscript places the clear direct feeding with Nocty after the Day 10 heading, at approximately 09:00. I am retaining Serena’s notation here as a cross-reference but recording the actual feeding under Day 10.

Energy state: Tired and hungry → Starving after sleep → Fed from Azrith. Exact final fullness not stated.

Carfaxx assessment: Sleep is not a substitute for feeding. Apparently this needed to be written down. Her body wanted both and, being Franzi, she attempted to negotiate with biology until biology won.

Erica: Not yet applicable.

Day 10 · 1 Dawnbloom

Franzi

◆ Morning, approximately 09:00 — Noctyralyn and Azrith · Direct group feeding. Franzi takes in sexual energy from both. This is the Nocty entry that Serena’s shorthand appears to attach to the previous night.

◆ Later day — Martha Moonpelt · Serena records Martha. By this point heavy artificing and rescue work have driven Franzi back into severe hunger. Martha provides a meaningful feeding and takes the edge off, but Franzi remains far from sated and describes herself as famished.

◆ Later day — General Michael · Serena also records Michael. Franzi turns to him because the earlier feeding has not been enough. Michael’s much stronger energy finally restores her substantially; Franzi explicitly thanks him for filling her up again.

Energy state: Fed in the morning → depleted again through exertion → Famished → Partially fed by Martha → Refilled by Michael.

Carfaxx assessment: Excellent demonstration of why counting partners is not the same as monitoring nourishment. She fed in the morning and still reached hunger severe enough to affect control later that day because she spent the intervening hours burning energy on artificing, Portals and rescue work. Intake must be judged against expenditure, not against the calendar.

Erica: Not yet applicable.

Days 11–12 | Running on Fumes and Commander Recovery

Carfaxx’s Feeding Ledger

Subject: Franziska Roseheart

Period: Days 11–12

Day 11 · 2 Dawnbloom

Franzi

◆ Night / Overnight — Velkris Nightgloom · Serena records Velkris. After rune work and another rescue, Franzi’s Succubus is explicitly running on fumes. Velkris provides a prolonged recovery feeding through the night. Franzi repeatedly feeds during the encounter, and the manuscript states that they eventually fall asleep after both are sated.

Energy state: Running on fumes / depleted → Sated.

Carfaxx assessment: Good recovery, poor timing. Reaching “fumes” before feeding is becoming a pattern rather than an accident. The fact that she can recover spectacularly does not make depletion a sensible operating procedure.

Erica: Not yet applicable.

Day 12 · 3 Dawnbloom

Franzi

◆ Night / Overnight — Serith Voidthorn · Serena records Serith. Franzi is already hungry before the day’s major work begins and finishes the operational day really tired and really hungry. Serith provides a long, deliberate recovery feeding with explicit limits and a stop signal agreed beforehand. The encounter continues into the early hours and includes substantial aftercare.

Energy state: Hungry early → Very hungry and exhausted by evening → Well fed / recovered enough to sleep. Exact level of fullness is not stated.

Carfaxx assessment: Two useful observations. First, again, she waits too long. Second, Serith handles limits and aftercare correctly, which matters when exhaustion and hunger are both high. The feeding clearly helps, but the manuscript does not give me grounds to mark her “fully sated”, so I will not manufacture certainty merely because it would make the ledger tidier.

Erica: Not yet applicable.
