THE NORTH

Old peoples, old powers and older claims.

The North lies beyond the great mountain systems north of the Demon Empire and is made up of distinct peoples and territories rather than a single conventional empire.

Political Landscape

The North is not organised like the Demon, Dragon or Fae Empires. Its political identity is distributed among its peoples, settlements and ancient northern powers.

Established northern peoples include Vikings, Valkyries, Frost Giants, Drow and Frost Dwarves, alongside old northern beings and the ancient presence of the North itself.

Established Centres

Hope's End: Viking settlement and northern Portal point.

Glaciarok: Frost Giant capital and Portal point.

Khar-Zelyth: capital of the Dökkálfar / Drow.

Svartalfheim: home of the Dvergar / Frost Dwarves.

Other established northern locations include the Grove Path, the Fossegrim waterfall and the Valkyrie plateau.
