THE DEMON EMPIRE

Old power, dangerous roads and more than one kind of monster.

The Demon Empire is vast, old and extraordinarily varied. Its politics are as complicated as its geography, and travellers who assume it is nothing but lava and ominous castles are likely to be surprised.

General Geography

The Empire borders the North beyond its great northern mountain ranges, the Fae Empire to the southwest and the Dragon Empire to the south and southeast. Its western boundary is the ocean. At Infernal Crossing, Demon, Fae and Dragon territory converge.

The Empire is conventionally divided into the Northern, Western, Southern and Eastern Territories, with Hellrath close to their meeting point. Beyond those administrative regions lie powerful duchies, marches, baronies, ancient House lands, frontier zones and territories whose geography is easier to define than their politics.

Hellrath is the central imperial capital, built on a black basalt plateau and home to roughly two million citizens.

The Great Landed Territories

Duchy of Korvale: one of the Empire's most important agricultural regions, producing wheat, corn, vegetables and fruit.

March of Bowmont: western coastal cattle country associated with Stormhaven, horses, leather and military supply.

March of Rhylas: rolling country north of Infernal Crossing, strategically important to the southern approaches.

March of Vellanor: the financial power base surrounding Mirehaven and associated with House Vellanor, banking and commerce.

Baronies of Highfield and d'Asteroth: renowned respectively for Dire Eagle breeding and prestigious wine production.

Shadow Marches: bogs, marshland, quicksand, bandits and near-perpetual twilight between Korvale and Bowmont.

Charred Vale and Outlying Expanse: unstable volcanic frontier country shaped by exile, warbands, mining and dangerous strongholds.

Political Shape

The four broad Territories are overseen by high-ranking nobles acting on behalf of the Demon Emperor while retaining their own hereditary power bases. The result is a central Empire with significant local authority, regional economies and House politics.

The northeast contains Roseheart lands and the sentient forest Ebon, whose political and metaphysical agency makes the region unlike ordinary landed territory. The east contains magical universities and mountain cities, while the south contains industrial, arcane and border regions leading toward Infernal Crossing.
