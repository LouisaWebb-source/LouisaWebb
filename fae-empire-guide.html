THE FAE EMPIRE

Six Major Courts. Infinite complications.

The Fae Empire is a federation of magical Courts beneath one imperial crown, where geography, politics and magic are unusually difficult to separate.

General Geography

The Fae Empire lies south of the Demon Empire, west of the Dragon Empire and north of the Celestial Dominion. Crossing from one Court into another can mean moving from frozen mountains into perpetual blossom, volcanic woodland, eternal twilight or floating islands inside a living storm.

The Six Major Courts

Ice Court: winter, precision, restraint and endurance; ruled from Aurefrost.

Moonlight Court: reflection, memory, dreams, stillness and secrecy; ruled from Silvershade Haven.

Blossom Court: life, growth, healing and renewal; ruled from Verdanthall.

Ashen Court: fire, transformation, destruction and renewal; ruled from Emberhall.

Court of Dawn: beauty, art, invention, illusion and enchantment; ruled from Luminara.

Storm Court: tempest, freedom, wind, rain and raw elemental power; ruled from Tempestspire.

Government and Minor Courts

Each Major Court has its own monarch, government, traditions, laws, magical specialisations and political interests. The Emperor stands above them in matters affecting the Empire as a whole but does not replace their individual governments.

The imperial crown is not hereditary. A candidate requires the favour of at least half of the six Major Courts.

Numerous Minor Courts, including the Musical, Crystal, Echoes, Perfume and Moonlit Courts, exist without provinces of their own and commonly align themselves with stronger Courts, Houses or political patrons.
