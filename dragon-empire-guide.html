THE DRAGON EMPIRE

Five great clans. One imperial crown.

The Dragon Empire is an imperial clan civilisation whose landscapes, elemental affinities, architecture, industries and politics are inseparable from the five great Dragon Clans.

General Geography

The Dragon Empire lies east of the Fae Empire and southeast of the Demon Empire. Its territory ranges from burning deserts and sun-scorched canyons to vineyard country, magically saturated valleys, arctic mountains and volcanic ranges.

Clan territory is more than an administrative division: geography, magical affinity, local culture and economic strengths have developed together over centuries.

The Five Great Clans

Clan Peskovyr: Sand Dragons.

Clan Zharokrylykh: Summer Dragons.

Clan Volkhoryn: Sorcerer Dragons.

Clan Morozhane: Frost Dragons.

Clan Krasogor: Mountain Dragons.

Each clan controls major territories and cities and retains significant regional, military and political authority beneath the Emperor.

Government

The Empire is ruled by Dragon Emperor Drago Nikolai Dragovich. Imperial policy is shaped alongside two advisory chambers.

The First Chamber — Clan Heads: representatives of the five great clans and their territorial, economic, military and political interests.

The Second Chamber — Circle of Eight: the Empire's magical chamber, led by First Dragon Wizard Grand Magister Kasimir Luthien and responsible for magical policy, wards, artefacts and arcane law.
